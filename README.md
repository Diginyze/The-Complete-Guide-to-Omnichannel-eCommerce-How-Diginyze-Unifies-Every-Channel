So What Does "Omnichannel" Actually Mean?

Here's the distinction that matters: being on multiple channels isn't the same as being omnichannel.

Multichannel just means you've got a presence everywhere  a website, an app, a store, maybe an Amazon listing. Omnichannel means those channels actually talk to each other. Same inventory numbers, same prices, same customer history, no matter where someone shows up. It's the difference between having five separate storefronts and having one business that happens to show up in five places.

The 6 Things Real Omnichannel Commerce Requires

Buy online, pick up in-store. Ship from the nearest store instead of a warehouse three states away. Return something bought online at a physical counter. These sound simple but require serious coordination behind the scenes  and Diginyze's order management system handles all of it out of the box.
